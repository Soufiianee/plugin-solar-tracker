## Rule used per state

- **CA** (UL 3700 / complete-system state): Pub. Util. Code 8530(c)(5)-(6): "certified as a plug-in photovoltaic system" by UL or equivalent NRTL, plus a certified isolation feature; ≤1,200 W per dwelling.
- **CO** (lab-listed state): C.R.S. 40-2-140(1)(e): ≤1,920 W to the grid; "labeled and listed by an NRTL"; one system per address; one device per outlet. From 1/1/2027 devices >391 W must be UL labeled and listed to be sold. Caveat: One device per outlet and one system per address.
- **CT** (lab-listed state): PA 26-127 Sec. 9(a): ≤1,200 W; 120 V outlet; "certified by UL or an equivalent NRTL, and meets UL 1741"; one device behind a meter. Caveat: One device behind the meter: multi-inverter kits (CraftStrom 2-3 inverters, several Hoymiles units) may count as more than one device.
- **MD** (lab-listed state): PUA 7-321(a): ≤1,200 W to the building; standard outlet; "certified by Underwriters Laboratory or an equivalent NRTL"; one per meter; notify utility first. Caveat: One system per meter; multi-inverter kits: confirm it counts as one system.
- **ME** (UL 3700 / complete-system state): 35-A MRSA 3475(1)(A): listed/certified to UL 3700 or a comparable NRTL standard, OR configured per the NEC adopted by the Electricians' Examining Board; ≤420 W no electrician, up to 1,200 W with electrician + dedicated circuit. Caveat: The "configured per the NEC" route is not modelled; we treat Maine like the UL 3700 states. Above 420 W an electrician and dedicated circuit are required.
- **NH** (unverified): Not verified (rules held as unknown).
- **NJ** (UL 3700 / complete-system state): S2368 Sec. 2(a): listed/certified to UL 3700, a comparable standard, or a combination covering plug-in safety and grid-parallel operation, by an NRTL; ≤1,200 W.
- **UT** (lab-listed state): Utah Code 54-15-102(13): ≤1,200 W output; standard 120 V outlet; NEC; "certified by UL or an equivalent NRTL".
- **VA** (lab-listed state): Va. Code 56-596.7(A): ≤1,200 W per customer or unit; certified by an NRTL (29 CFR 1910.7); NEC; SCC notice form before installing.
- **VT** (UL 3700 / complete-system state): 30 V.S.A. 201(9)(C): "complies with UL 3700" (or equivalent NRTL certification) and IEEE 1547-2018; ≤1,200 W per meter; smart meter only.

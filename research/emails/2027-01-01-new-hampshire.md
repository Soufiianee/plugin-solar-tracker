**Subject:** New Hampshire's plug-in solar law starts today

**Preview text:** What SB 540 allows, and where kit certification stands.

**Body (plain text):**

New Hampshire's plug-in solar law, SB 540-FN (Chapter 262, Laws of 2026), takes effect today, January 1, 2027.

What it allows: each metered customer may use one plug-in solar system with an inverter output of up to 1,200 watts (AC). The system must be certified by a nationally recognized testing laboratory to meet nationally recognized safety standards applicable to distributed energy resources and plug-in solar generation systems. No specific UL standard is named. Your utility cannot require approval, an interconnection agreement or fees. It may set up a way for you to report the system's wattage after you install it, for information only.

The law does not mention landlords or HOAs, so your lease and your association's rules still apply.

Kits: no kit has a complete-system certificate we have seen. For APsystems EZ1-LV plug-in microinverter we have seen a lab certificate for the inverter, so we mark it "Likely compatible: confirm with seller". Ask the seller to confirm that the whole kit is certified before you buy.

New Hampshire rules and sources: https://balconysolarrules.com/states/new-hampshire/

This is information, not legal advice. Check the law and your lease or HOA rules before you install anything.
